Garment Preservation

Caring for Your Tailored Clothing

Suit & Coat Storage

Always hang tailored suits on wide contoured wooden hangers to preserve shoulder structure. Allow wool garments to rest 24 hours between wears.

Cleaning & Steam

Dry clean tailored garments sparingly. Use light hand steaming to clear wrinkles and refresh natural textile fibers without harsh chemicals.

Traditional Care

Iron traditional kurtas and shalwars inside out on medium heat. Avoid heavy pressure directly over custom embroidery or delicate buttons.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment? +

You can book an appointment directly through our online request form above or send us a WhatsApp message at 03093269261 to arrange a personal consultation session.

What measurements are required? +

For bespoke garments, we prefer taking 15+ detailed body measurements in person at our studio. If ordering remotely, you can provide measurements following our online guide.

Can I bring my own fabric? +

Yes, absolutely. You are welcome to bring your own unstitched fabric, or choose from our curated house collection of premium wools, cottons, and silks.

Do you offer alterations? +

Yes, we perform alterations on suits, trousers, dresses, and ethnic garments to ensure your existing wardrobe fits perfectly.

How long does tailoring usually take? +

Standard custom tailoring takes between 7 to 10 days including a fitting session. Express tailoring options are available upon request for time-sensitive occasions.
